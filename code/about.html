Our story

Built on trust,
grown with purpose.

Eco Hospitality began with one simple belief: a beautiful stay should give something back to the place that makes it possible.

From humble beginnings in Himachal Pradesh, we have grown by bringing together thoughtful comfort, responsible operations and the warmth of local hospitality. Every property is different, but the intention remains the same—to help guests feel more connected to the landscape, the culture and the people who call it home.

What guides us

Care that reaches

Sustainability, community and guest experience are not separate ideas here. They are parts of one enduring promise.

A green commitment

Design that belongs
to the landscape

Our properties work with their surroundings rather than against them. We continually reduce waste, use resources mindfully and choose practices that help preserve the forests, water and mountain ecosystems around us.

Enriching communities

Local knowledge,
shared with pride.

The soul of Himachal lives in its people, traditions and stories. Through local employment, skill development and long-term partnerships, we help create opportunity while celebrating the culture that makes each destination distinct.

Guests as partners

Every stay leaves
a quieter footprint

Our guests are part of the movement. Each stay is an invitation to slow down, connect with nature and make more conscious choices—at our properties and long after returning home.
