Welcome to Golden Fern Resort

Golden Fern Resort Shimla
by Eco Hospitality

Golden Fern Resort Shimla is ideally located on Shimla Kalka Highway at Kachi Ghati, just 5 km away from the city center, The Mall Road. As one of the top luxury resort in Shimla, it offers 42 elegantly appointed rooms and suites, designed to provide modest luxury and stylish class with modern furnishings, LED TV, Mini Bar, Lockers, WiFi, and more. The Resort features a Banquet Hall and Conference Hall, making it an ideal venue for events, weddings, and corporate conferences in Shimla. All rooms are valley-facing, allowing guests to soak in the Alpine splendor from their private balconies. Soothe your tired muscles with the jacuzzi, gym and rejuvenate yourself in the Queen of Hills. Whether you are seeking relaxation or adventure, Golden Fern Resort stands out among luxury resort in Shimla, offering an exceptional stay with warm hospitality. The in-house restaurant serves Indian, Chinese, and Continental cuisines, along with traditional local delicacies.
