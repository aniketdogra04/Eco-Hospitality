The Spa

Rest is part of the journey

Planning a destination wedding becomes special when the venue itself feels like part of the celebration, and Golden Fern Resort delivers exactly that. As a wedding venue in Shimla, it offers luxury without compromise. Overlooking peaceful valleys, this luxury hill-station resort offers everything couples look for in a dream destination wedding: panoramic outdoor venues, elegant banquet spaces, and stay-and-celebrate convenience for all your guests. What makes Golden Fern truly unique is its flexible indoor & open-air wedding setups and complete ceremony support from mandap designs to curated menus. In a place where every ritual is set against the mountains, Golden Fern Resort transforms your wedding into an experience filled with charm, comfort, and effortless hospitality.
