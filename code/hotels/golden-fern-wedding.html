Ideal venue

The ideal wedding
venue in Shimla

Golden Fern Resort blends Himalayan scenery with practical comfort, making it the ideal wedding venue in Shimla. Located on the Shimla–Kalka Highway, 5 km from Mall Road, the resort offers 42 valley-facing rooms and suites with private balconies that invite quiet moments between ceremonies. Two elegant banquet halls, a restaurant terrace, and an expansive lawn (10,000 sq. ft) to host ceremonies and receptions for over 500 people. In-house services include catering, décor, professional DJs, bridal dressing rooms, and wellness amenities, ensuring guest comfort and easy logistics. Scenic corners across the property provide natural backdrops for photography, and the events team collaborates closely with families to honor Himachali traditions and personal touches. Golden Fern turns every wedding into a balanced celebration of ritual, hospitality, and mountain beauty while being a wedding venue in Shimla
