About Shimla

Old-world grace,
a wilder heart

Shimla unfolds in layers: slate roofs and forest ridges, lively bazaars and long silences beneath the deodars.

Once the summer capital of British India, Shimla is today the capital of Himachal Pradesh: a ridge-top town of timbered facades, café-lined promenades and deodar forests that fall away into blue valleys.

Come for a long weekend of strolling and slow meals, or use it as your gateway to the wider Himalayas.
